How big are studies?

Enrollment is the number of participants a study involves. The registry reports it two ways, and the difference matters: Estimated is the sponsor's target, usually recorded while a study is still planned or running, while Actual is the number the sponsor reports having enrolled. Comparing the two — and comparing studies still underway against those already closed — shows both how large studies really are and how far ambition drifts from outcome.
